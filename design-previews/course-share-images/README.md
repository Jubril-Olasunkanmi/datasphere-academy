# Course share-image redesign

The three initial compositions were rendered and reviewed:

- `concept-a.png` — Editorial Minimalism: asymmetric title/art composition, quiet information row, restrained CTA.
- `concept-b.png` — Contemporary Education: structured image-first layout with a stronger content frame.
- `concept-c.png` — Bold Typographic: navy-led composition with high-contrast type and compact artwork.

Concept A was selected because it gave the course title the clearest hierarchy, preserved the illustration as the second focal point, stayed readable at small sizes, and used the fewest visual elements.

The `final-*.png` files are the five course samples. `qa-data-analytics-540.png` and `qa-data-analytics-320.png` are the small-size review passes.
